# Content & Conversion Strategy: RomeroDev

This document describes the B2B content marketing strategy, services positioning, and conversion funnel designed for the RomeroDev portfolio.

---

## 1. Target Audience & Positioning

RomeroDev is positioned as a senior software and product engineering agency led by Lester Romero Bernardo. We target three specific client profiles:
1. **SaaS Founders & Startups:** Need native iOS development or UX/UI product design to bring their MVP or product version 2.0 to life.
2. **Medium-to-Large Enterprises:** Need Salesforce CRM optimization, advanced Apex/LWC development, or secure integrations with ERP systems like SAP.
3. **Product Teams:** Need external technical audits to solve performance debt, memory leaks, or accessibility (WCAG) issues in SwiftUI or web applications.

---

## 2. Core Service Pillars

We define 5 specialized service pages, each built with high technical clarity and strong conversion hooks:
1. **Desarrollo iOS Nativo:** Focuses on premium SwiftUI/Swift development, lockscreen widgets, and HealthKit integrations.
2. **Consultoría Salesforce:** Targets corporate CRM efficiency, custom flows, and data architecture.
3. **Auditoría de Aplicaciones:** Targets technical debt reduction, memory leak discovery, and performance tuning.
4. **Diseño de Producto:** Emphasizes Apple's HIG (Human Interface Guidelines), Figma wireframing, and user discovery.
5. **Integración y Automatización:** Solves complex, secure data pipelines between Salesforce and SAP/ERPs.

Each page follows a structured layout: Hero → Pain Points → Included Solutions → Process Steps → Deliverables → NDA Confidentiality Notice (for Salesforce/Enterprise) → Accordion FAQ → Contextual CTA.

---

## 3. Knowledge Lab Content Strategy (Resources)

To build organic search authority and establish technical trust, we launched the **Knowledge Lab** with 5 initial deep-dive articles:
1. **Rendimiento de SwiftUI (Listas):** Targets iOS developers and technical leads. Solves list lags and rendering loops.
2. **Deuda Técnica en Salesforce:** Targets CRM managers and CIOs. Focuses on identifying redundant triggers and workflow creep.
3. **Integración Salesforce-SAP:** Targets enterprise architects. Reviews secure REST/SOAP patterns and middle-layer authentication.
4. **Guía de Lanzamiento en App Store:** Targets founders. Explains provisioning profiles, metadata preparation, and pre-release checklists.
5. **Diseño iOS según Apple HIG:** Targets designers and product managers. Breaks down navigation layouts, lockscreen widget constraints, and watchOS design.

Every article ends with a customized call-to-action box encouraging readers to book an architectural audit or consultation.

---

## 4. Case Studies & Social Proof

Our products, **VitalsPath** (family health companion) and **StreakReps** (gym routine planner), serve as live proof of our technical capabilities.
* **Problem-Benefit Framing:** The case study details are structured as "Challenge vs. Solution".
* **ASO Screenshots:** Clean, localized captions instead of filenames prove design discipline and care.
* **Apple Health integration:** Highlights the capabilities of working with sensor and health databases securely.
